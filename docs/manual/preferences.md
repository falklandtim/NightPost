# Preferences

Open Preferences with ⌘ , (Ctrl , on Linux). It has six pages.

## Appearance

- **Theme:** **Light**, **Dark**, or **Match system**, which follows your computer's light or dark setting.
- **Show emails as designed (HTML):** shows emails with their own layout. Turn it off to see plain text.
- **Load remote images (known trackers are blocked):** when it's off, pictures from the web wait until you click **Load images** on an email.
- **Match emails to a dark theme:** recolours emails to suit a dark theme. Turn it off to see them on white, as designed.
- **Show sender photos and logos:** photos from your Contacts (on the Mac) and companies' logos, instead of coloured letters.

See [Reading mail](reading.md) for more on these.

## Message list

- **Swipe actions:** up to three buttons for swiping right and for swiping left. The first is what a full swipe does; set it to **Nothing** to turn that side off. See [Organising mail](organising.md#swipes).
- **Quick actions:** buttons on the conversation you point at, once the list is at least 420 points wide.
- **Undo Send:** how long a sent message waits before it goes (5 to 60 seconds), or **Off** to send straight away.

## Signatures

Make, edit and delete signatures. Each has a name, and can be plain text or HTML. Choose a default signature for each account here too. See [Writing mail](writing.md#signatures).

## Accounts

- Each account, with how it gets new mail: **instant (push)**, **reconnecting**, or **every 5 minutes**.
- **Edit** changes an account's details. **Remove** takes it out of NightPost, but leaves its mail on the server.
- **Use Google sign-in** switches a Gmail account from an app password to signing in through Google. **Sign in again** appears when that sign-in has stopped working.
- **+ Add Account** adds another.
- **Add demo accounts** or **Remove demo accounts**, for the made-up demo mailbox.
- **Sign in with Google:** where you enter your Google OAuth client, needed before Google sign-in works. See [Adding your accounts](accounts.md#gmail-and-google-workspace).
