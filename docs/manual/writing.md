# Writing mail

## New messages, replies and forwards

- **New message:** press `c`, or click **New message**.
- **Reply** (`r`), **Reply all** (`a`) or **Forward** (`f`): use the keys, or the toolbar buttons on an open conversation.

Each message opens in its own window. Choose which account it's sent from with **From**.

**To, Cc and Bcc** suggest addresses as you type. People you've written to come first, then people who've written to you, then, on the Mac, your Contacts. Use ↑ ↓ and Return or Tab to pick one, or click it.

## Formatting

The toolbar above the text has fonts and sizes, bold, italic, underline and strikethrough, text colour and highlight, bulleted and numbered lists, quotes, alignment and **Clear formatting**.

- **Links:** select some text and press ⌘ K.
- **Pictures in the text:** insert one from the toolbar, paste it, or drag it in. It's sent inside the email.
- **Spelling** is checked as you type, using your computer's dictionaries. On Linux, install a Hunspell dictionary (for example `hunspell-en`) if you don't see any.

Messages are sent as formatted HTML, with a plain-text version for mail apps that want one. Replies quote the earlier message.

## Attachments

Click **Attach**, or drag files onto the message window. Each file shows as a chip with its size, and a thumbnail for photos; × removes it.

When you attach JPEG or PNG photos, an **Image size** menu offers **Actual Size**, **Large** (2048 pixels), **Medium** (1280) or **Small** (640), measured along the longest side. Photos are resized for you and turned the right way up, and each chip shows the new size next to the original.

The window shows the message's total size. Gmail and iCloud won't take messages over 25 MB, so NightPost won't send one that big.

## Signatures

Make your signatures in **Preferences → Signatures**, and choose a default for each account. Each one can be plain text or HTML. You can paste a signature copied from another app; NightPost keeps the signature and drops anything that would restyle the rest of your email.

In the message window, the signature menu swaps between your signatures. Changing the **From** account switches to that account's default.

## Drafts

While you write, NightPost saves the message to the account's Drafts folder by itself, 3 seconds after you stop typing. Press ⌘ S to save at once. The bottom of the window shows when it was last saved. Because drafts are on the server, your phone and other mail apps see them too.

- **Closing the window** keeps the message as a draft.
- **Discard** deletes it.
- A conversation with one of your drafts in it shows **Edit draft**, which reopens it with its recipients, attachments and signature.

## Sending, and Undo Send

Press **Send**, or ⌘ Return. The message then waits 10 seconds before it goes, with **Undo** and **Send now** at the bottom of the main window. **Undo** (or ⌘ Z) reopens it so you can carry on writing.

Change the wait, or turn it off, in **Preferences → Message list → Undo Send**. If you quit NightPost during the wait, the message is sent first; if that fails, it's saved to Drafts.
