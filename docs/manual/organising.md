# Organising mail

Everything on this page works on the open conversation, or on several selected at once (see [Several conversations at once](#several-conversations-at-once)).

## Archive, trash and spam

- **Archive** (`e`, or the ✓ button) takes a conversation out of the Inbox, but keeps it. On Gmail it just loses its Inbox label, so its other labels stay.
- **Move to Trash** (`#` or Delete, or the bin button).
- **Report as spam** (`!`).

NightPost never deletes mail outright: trashed mail goes to the account's Trash folder, as it would in any mail app.

## Undo

Almost everything can be undone: press ⌘ Z, or click **Undo** on the message that appears at the bottom of the window. That covers archive, trash, spam, move, labels, snooze, pin, star, read and unread, and sending (see [Writing mail](writing.md)). Moves are reversed on the server too.

## Swipes

Swipe a conversation sideways with two fingers on a trackpad, or drag it sideways with the mouse.

- **A short swipe** opens the row on its buttons. Click one, or click anywhere else to close the row again.
- **A full swipe**, more than about half the row, does the first button straight away.

Unless you change them:

| Swipe | Buttons | A full swipe |
|---|---|---|
| Left | **Trash**, **Snooze**, **More** (the right-click menu) | moves it to Trash |
| Right | **Archive**, **Read** or **Unread** | archives it |

You can choose up to three buttons for each side in **Preferences → Message list**, from Archive, Trash, Snooze, Move to…, More, Pin, Read/Unread, Star, Spam and Reply. To turn one side off, set its first button to **Nothing**.

## Buttons on hover

When the message list is wide enough (at least 420 points; drag its edge to widen it), pointing at a conversation shows buttons for mark read or unread, archive, trash, snooze and pin. Turn them off in Preferences → Message list.

## Moving to a folder

- Press `v`, click the folder button in the toolbar, or right-click → **Move to**.
- Start typing the folder's name, choose it with ↑ ↓, and press Return.
- If the folder doesn't exist yet, you can create it from the same list.

You can also **drag** a conversation up or down to pick it up, then drop it on a folder in the sidebar. Dropping it on **All Accounts → Inbox, Archive, Spam or Trash** works whichever account it's in.

**Moving to another account:** the Move list also shows your other accounts' main folders. Dropping a conversation on another account copies it there, and puts the originals in the first account's Trash. This one can't be undone with ⌘ Z.

## Gmail labels

On Gmail, a conversation can carry several labels. Press `l`, click the tag button, or right-click → **Label**, then pick labels the same way as folders. Labels show as chips on the open conversation; click × on a chip to remove it.

## Making and deleting folders

Right-click an account's name for **New folder…**, or one of its folders for **New folder inside…** and **Delete folder…**.

Deleting asks first. On Gmail, the label goes but its mail stays in All Mail. On other servers, the folder's mail goes to Trash first. Inbox, Sent, Drafts, Trash and the other standard folders can't be deleted.

## Pins

Pin a conversation to keep it above everything else, in every list it appears in: press `p`, click the pin in the toolbar, or right-click → **Pin to top**. Pinned conversations have a purple tint, deeper when they hold unread mail. Press `p` again to unpin.

## Snooze

Snooze a conversation to hide it until later: press `b`, or click the clock in the toolbar. Choose:

- **Later today, Tonight, Tomorrow, This weekend, Next week** or **Next month**;
- **Pick a date & time**, with a calendar;
- or type a time, such as `3h`, `friday` or `2026-12-01 09:00`.

The conversation leaves every list. At that time it comes back to the top of your Inbox, marked unread. **Snoozed** in the sidebar lists what's waiting; open one and snooze it again to change the time, or unsnooze it.

Snoozes are kept on the mail server, so every computer running NightPost agrees. Your phone's mail app won't show snoozed mail in its Inbox either. See [Using NightPost on several computers](several-computers.md).

## Star, read and unread

- `s` stars or unstars a conversation.
- `u` marks it unread.
- Opening a conversation marks it read.

## Several conversations at once

- ⌘-click adds a conversation to the selection, or takes it out.
- Shift-click selects everything between.
- Shift ↑ or ↓ extends the selection.
- ⌘ A selects the whole list.

Archive, Trash, Spam, Move, Label, Snooze, Pin, Star and read or unread then apply to them all: from the toolbar, the keyboard, or the summary in the reading pane. One ⌘ Z undoes the lot. Move and Label need the conversations to be in one account, since each account has its own folders. Press Esc to clear the selection.
